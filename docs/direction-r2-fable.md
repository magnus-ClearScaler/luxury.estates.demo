## 0. What the owner said, and what it means

"The best houses are not on the first page. The best pictures are the beautiful pictures of the villas. The first two houses on the home page are not the best villas. Les Pins does not have many beautiful pictures of the house. Maison Edouard has an insane view but not enough photos of the house. Link the front page to the best pictures of the houses, and add some more houses from online."

He is right on every count. Today Home shows Les Pins 01 (pines, no house), Villa Blanche 10 (a front door), Maison Édouard 02 (pool and sea, no house) and Le Belvédère 01 (a deck). Only two of the six Home stills contain a house. The index opens on the two weakest sets of the nine.

The rule from here: **every still on Home is a photograph of a house, whole, in its setting.** A view without a house is secondary everywhere and never a Home feature. Houses without a strong house frame are not on Home and sit at the foot of the index or are held. Everything the owner likes stays: the sea film, the palette, the type, the inset prints, the spacing, the sailboat.
