# Photo scores: how beautiful is each house, as a house

Owner round 2 (2026-10-09): the home page must lead with the most beautiful photographs of the villas themselves. A view without the house is secondary. This file scores the photography of all 14 source folders in `assets/originals/listings/` against that bar: would you fall in love with the villa from this photo?

Method: every frame in every folder was looked at on a labelled contact sheet (scratchpad `photoscore/<slug>_p1.jpg`, `_p2.jpg`), and the strongest candidates were also checked as 100% centre crops for sharpness and artefacts. Score is 0 to 10 for the best photos of the house itself, not for views, interiors or the price of the house. Paths are relative to `assets/originals/listings/`.

## House by house

### Mas Blanc, Mougins: 8.5
- **01**: the single best house photograph in the set. Dark green pool in the foreground reflecting the house, wide stone steps rising between lavender, cypress and olive to a long whitewashed mas with arched windows, pergola and bougainvillea. Calm, natural colour, sharp at 100%.
- **02**: drone on the axis: pool, deck, steps, the house under tall pines with blue hills behind. The whole estate in one frame, and still calm.
- **07**: the pergola terrace with the white house behind; a good secondary frame.
- Weakness: only two true exteriors. 03 to 06 and 08 are interiors (good, warm, uncluttered).

### Villa Blanche, Cap d'Antibes: 8.5
- **02**: the whole Belle Époque villa, ironwork balconies and columns, pool in front, umbrella pines framing it. The archetype of a Cap d'Antibes villa.
- **03**: three-quarter view with the lantern tower and pool running into the pines. Crops well to portrait.
- **05**: frontal colonnade and balcony straight across the pool. Formal, symmetrical, good for a wide band.
- **01**: drone with the house among pines and the sea beyond. 06 and 10 (pool corner, entrance stair) are detail frames.
- Weakness: cyan pool and deep blue sky need pulling back in the grade; a thatched pool bar sits at left in 02, 03 and 06 (crop it out); 07 is a stone outbuilding, not the villa; interiors 08, 09 are heavy Louis-style.

### Bastide des Prés, Châteauneuf-Grasse: 7.5, with a caveat
- **11**: stone bastide with green shutters behind two plane trees, the formal pond in front. **01**: the same view, wider, with the pond in the foreground. **02**: ivy-covered stone facade and the terrace table, close and lovely. **12**: drone of house, cypress, arcaded pool in meadow. **09**: pool against the arched stone wall (house not visible). **06**: entrance door detail.
- Caveat: at 100% the stone in 01, 02, 11 has a painterly, smeared texture and the foliage melts, and the interiors 03 to 05 look virtually staged. These read as AI-enhanced or AI-staged listing photos. At display width (up to about 1200 px) 01 and 11 hold up, but do not blow them to full-bleed 1440 and do not lean on the interiors. 07 to 10 and 12 look like straightforward photographs.

### Domaine des Vignes, Saint-Tropez: 7.5
- **01**: stone-tiled pool in the foreground, terraced lavender garden and twin stone stairs, the long low Provençal house under umbrella pines. The calmest, most natural colour in the whole set.
- **04**: drone over plunge pool, pool house and the main house among pines and lawn. **03**: one wing of the house along the lawn under umbrella pines.
- Weakness: the house sits small and far back in every frame; 02 is lawn only.

### Bastide du Ciel, Gassin (held): 7.5
- **02**: ochre bastide with terracotta roofs between a cypress and an umbrella pine, the Gulf of Saint-Tropez behind. One of the five best frames across all houses.
- **01**: the house low in its garden under a great umbrella pine. **03**: vine-pergola facade over the lawn. **04**: shuttered courtyard with the table under a parasol.
- Weakness: slightly flat HDR look and wide-angle; interiors (07, 08) dated. Worth publishing for its exteriors alone.

### Bastide de la Rade, Villefranche-sur-Mer (held): 7
- **01**: stone bastide with round tower, white shutters, arcaded terrace and bougainvillea. Strong portrait candidate. **04**: the facade seen through pines. **09**: tower, chimney and timber balconies between cypresses. **02, 03**: the tower over the bay of Villefranche (house partly visible).
- Weakness: the most HDR-heavy set: saturated sky, pumped greens, converging verticals. Needs a firm desaturating grade to sit beside Mas Blanc. Interiors 10, 11 dated.

### Maison des Collines, Ramatuelle: 6
- **09**: flat-roofed modern house stacked above a dry-stone wall, agapanthus and pool. **01**: drone of the long low house, pool and young vines under the pine hill. **03, 02**: house beside the lawn and the long pool.
- Weakness: contemporary architecture reads less romantic than the bastides; strong wide-angle on 02, 03; a lot of lawn.

### Domaine des Cyprès, Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat (held): 6

### Maison Édouard, Beaulieu-sur-Mer: 5

## Gap for new houses

Even the best sets have only two to six true exteriors. New houses should be chosen for at least four calm frames that show the whole house in its garden: a stone bastide or mas among olives and cypress, a Belle Époque villa among pines, a pool in front of the facade, natural colour without HDR.

### House by house

#### Bastide des Oliviers, Vence: 8.5
- **01**: the 1751 bastide seen across its lawn, the whole facade under ivy and wisteria, old olives either side, the long pool and its parasols at far left, the hills behind. The most romantic house photograph in either batch. 1800x1013, so wide only (it will not crop to portrait).
- **02**: long pool in the foreground, the pool house and the bastide's stone gable behind it among olives, cypress and tall pines. Pool reads cyan; pull it back in the grade.
- **03**: the vine pergola walk along the house, stone benches, white shutters, the wooden door. A lovely detail or portrait crop.
- **04**: from under the pool house loggia across the pool to the olive park; house not the subject, but calm. 07 is a top-down drone (roofs only), 05 a garden view, 06 and 08 interiors (08, the vaulted salon with arched windows, is very good).
- At 100%: genuine photographs, sharp, slight vibrance lift on the greens; no AI texture.

#### Les Arcades, Ramatuelle: 8.5
- **02**: on the axis of the stone path between clipped hedges and cypress to the ivy-covered facade and its arched door. Symmetrical, calm, natural light. A perfect wide feature or band.
- **01**: drone of the whole white house and its wings among olives and cypress, umbrella pines and the open sea beyond. Crop the solar panels and car court off the bottom edge.
- **03**: the house small among great umbrella pines, the lawn and pool below it. Kidney-shaped pool is a little dated.
- **07**: white wing with arched loggias and a scalloped eave above yuccas; **05**: arcaded wing under palms and pines (palms cut the frame). 04 and 08 are the loggias looking out to the sea, 06 the infinity pool and a single umbrella pine over the sea (beautiful, but a view).
- At 100%: 02 is slightly soft (light upscale) but clean; 01 is sharp. The softest, least saturated colour of the new batch, closest to the site palette.

#### Villa Camille, Roquebrune-Cap-Martin: 8
- **02**: the 1891 villa in portrait, the full facade with balustraded steps and loggia framed by an olive branch and bougainvillea, a glimpse of sea at right. A natural portrait feature (1750x1864).
- **01**: the facade from the forecourt, turret, balconies, columns and the curved stair; very grand, but a strong deep blue sky and some wide-angle lean.
- **03**: the villa above its pool terrace with a clipped cypress; the pool is bright cyan and there is a diving board and two lamp posts, so card size only.
- **06**: drone with the white villa small in the foreground and Monaco and the coast beyond. **04**: the villa on its slope among neighbouring houses (shows the setting is built-up). 05, 07, 08 are terrace and Monaco views; 09, 10 dated interiors.
- At 100%: crisp, genuine. Deepen nothing; pull the sky down.

#### Maison du Chemin, Saint-Tropez: 8
- **06**: the second house seen across its lawn and pool under two old oaks, arched windows and balcony, a vine pergola. Calm and complete; the best frame of the set.
- **02**: the main facade behind the trunk of an old tree, arched steel windows, shutters, agapanthus. Intimate and beautiful; a portrait crop works on the left two thirds.
- **03**: the long pool on the lawn with both houses behind (wide-angle; the parasols and loungers at left are heavy). **01**: three-quarter view of the main house with its loggia and the pool beyond, white agapanthus in front. **05**: long pool in front of the main house. **04**: balcony and loggia close. **07**: the table under wisteria with the pool and garden.
- Weakness: new build (2024), so it lacks the patina of the bastides; greens are lifted and 01, 03 are wide-angle. 10 is a top-down drone; 08, 09 bright interiors.
- At 100%: sharp, genuine.

#### Le Mas Rose, Ramatuelle: 7.5
- **01**: the rose-washed house with sage shutters behind its dark green-tiled pool, a tall cypress and great umbrella pines around. Natural, quiet colour; the pool is green, not cyan. A strong band or row image.
- **04**: drone of the house, pool and vines in an unbroken pine landscape running to the sea. The house sits small but the setting is the point.
- **02**: house and pool closer, wide-angle; **05**: the pool with a cypress pair, house only at left edge. 03 and 06 are the reed pergola and table; 07 to 09 interiors.
- At 100%: genuine, slightly soft. Weakness: wide-angle in 02, 05; a lot of loungers.

#### La Pinède, Ramatuelle: 7
- **01**: stone and render house with wisteria terrace above a curving pool among umbrella pines and cypress. A lovely house, but the heaviest HDR of the batch: cyan pool, pumped greens, very blue sky. **03**: drone of the house and kidney pool in a wood of umbrella pines, hills behind.
- **06**: the dry-stone wing with arched windows under wisteria (fence in front, out-of-focus foreground). **02**: stone entrance with an old olive trunk. **04**: the wisteria terrace and long table. **11**: drone over the house to the vineyards and sea.
- Weakness: kidney pool with waterfall reads 1990s resort; needs a firm desaturating grade. 08 (infinity edge under pines) is a view.

#### Maison du Cap, Roquebrune-Cap-Martin: 6
- **03**: the contemporary stone and white house above its pale pool and terrace; **01**: drone of the house stacked on the slope with the pool; **02**: the low white guest wing in an olive and lavender garden.
- Weakness: contemporary, built-up neighbours in 01 and 03, artificial lawn, terraced white walls. Calm colour, but not a house to fall for. 04, 06, 07 are views over Menton bay; 05, 08 interiors.

#### La Pergola, Beaulieu-sur-Mer: 4.5
